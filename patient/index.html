No one is reading this

Nothing here is sent to the doctor's office, to a server, or to anyone else. It stays on this device, and nobody sees it until you show it to your doctor at an appointment. If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988, call 911, or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not wait for the appointment.

Setup

A first name or nickname is plenty. Don't enter a full name or birthdate — this works fine without one.

What to track

Turn off anything that doesn't apply. Shorter is better — a few minutes once a week is the difference between using this and quietly abandoning it.

Your data

Saved in this browser on this device only. Clearing browser data, switching browsers, or getting a new phone will lose it. Back up now and then.

How to use this

Fill this in once a week. Pick a day that's easy to remember — Sunday evening works for most people — and answer for the week as a whole.

If you miss a week, skip it and pick up the current one rather than filling it in from memory. A blank week is honest; a guessed one isn't, and it'll show up as a real number your doctor takes seriously.

There is no right answer and nothing here is a test. A 3 in a rough week is more useful than a 6 you didn't mean.

Before your appointment, open Summary and either print it or hand your doctor the phone. The point of this is the conversation it starts.
